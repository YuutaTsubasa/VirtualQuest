# Claude → Codex：其他五種畫風（PC-98、動漫電影、台式印刷、MS-DOS、Game Boy）

使用者看過 anime90 全部接好的版本，說「可以畫其它風格的圖了」，而且要**五種都做、分批交**。這份是總需求，五種照下面的順序一種一種做；每種再分成幾包，做好一包就交一包。

## 已經有、不用畫的

- **半身像**（11 位 × 5 表情）：直接沿用虛擬台麻館的各風格版本（衣服就是麻將館那套），我已經放進 `public/styles/<風格>/characters/`。動作圖、敵人、背景請照這些半身像的畫法與配色。
- **特效**（hit-spark、sand-puff、score-burst）：沿用虛擬排球王的各風格版本。
- **Logo**：沿用 UI Concept v1 的 Logo，不用另外畫；**只有 gameboy 要一張四色版** `ui/logo.png`（放在 gameboy 的 C 包，1400×560，邏輯 350×140 ×4）。現在遊戲裡是用濾鏡壓成綠色頂著。

## 畫風描述（貼進提示詞的 Style 段）

- **pc98**：Period PC-98 anime pixel art. Deliberate crisp pixel clusters, clean stepped pixel contours, elegant ~16-color indexed palette, restrained ordered/checkerboard dithering for transitions. Muted indigo/plum shadows, teal greens, warm cream highlights, restrained rose accents. Visible handcrafted pixel construction everywhere including faces; looks like original period game art, not a painting with a filter. No soft gradients or blur. **每張 ≤15 色＋透明。**
- **modern-paint**：Contemporary cinematic anime film / key-art painting. Recognizable anime drawing with nuanced painterly light, confident visible brush strokes, sophisticated warm/cool color variation, believable materials, luminous sky and backlight. Crisp focal edges, softer interior brushwork. NOT flat cel shading, NOT photorealistic, NOT 3D, no excessive bloom.
- **taiwan-print**：Taiwanese vintage hand-printed movie poster / shop-sign illustration. Confident coarse dark ink contours, deliberately flattened shapes, limited ink palette of deep jade green, vermilion red, warm ochre, ivory and muted teal blue. Subtle ink trapping (misregistration) and halftone dots in shadows, tiny paper grain INSIDE the artwork only. NOT a cel-animation recolor, NOT pixel art, NOT shiny 3D.
- **msdos**：MS-DOS VGA adventure/arcade game pixel art. Strong outer outlines, saturated flat color blocks, explicit palette quantization, grey bevelled 3D UI frames and deep blue panels, bright yellow selection. No blurred interpolation. **每張 ≤63 色＋透明。**
- **gameboy**：Original Game Boy (DMG) 4-shade monochrome green pixel art. **Exactly four opaque colors #0f380f #306230 #8bac0f #9bbc0f plus fully transparent; alpha 只有 0／255**; no gradients; chunky square pixel outlines, flat high-contrast panels. Draw it as Game Boy art from scratch — not an anime image reduced to green.

每一張都加：True transparent RGBA background (characters/props), no backdrop or checkerboard, no text/labels/watermark, small transparent margin on every side.
